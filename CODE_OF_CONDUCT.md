# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We — the maintainers and contributors of **Programming Zero To Hero** — pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project exists primarily for people who are **learning to code**. Please remember that at some point every one of us was a beginner.

---

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that **helps** build a positive community:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being patient with questions, especially from first-time contributors.
- Giving constructive, respectful code review feedback.
- Explaining the *why* behind suggestions, not just the *what*.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism yourself.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the learner.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

### Examples of behavior that **will not be tolerated**:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information (physical or email address) without explicit permission ("doxxing").
- Dismissive or condescending behavior toward beginners ("just Google it", "RTFM", etc.).
- Copying another contributor's PR code and submitting it as your own.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages).
- Any official community channels (if/when they are created).
- Public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

It also applies when an individual is **officially representing** the project in public — for example, posting a solution from this project on social media.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainer:

- **GitHub:** [@Blackvivek](https://github.com/Blackvivek)
- **Open a confidential issue** by pinging the maintainer directly in an issue or PR comment.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to:

1. **Warn** — a private message explaining what was wrong.
2. **Edit or delete** comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
3. **Temporarily ban** a contributor from the project for repeated or serious violations.
4. **Permanently ban** a contributor for severe harassment or when the above actions have not resolved the issue.

Decisions will be made with as much transparency as possible without compromising anyone's privacy.
